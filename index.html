How the System Works

A four-stage pipeline that handles everything from first contact to a qualified patient in your calendar — automatically.

1

Capture

AI-optimised landing pages and intake forms collect patient interest 24 hours a day across web, mobile, and social channels.

2

Qualify

Automated scoring filters leads by condition, urgency, location, and insurance eligibility before they ever reach your team.

3

Nurture

Personalised email and SMS sequences keep prospects engaged and informed while moving them toward a booked appointment.

4

Convert

Qualified leads are passed directly to your scheduling system with full context — ready to be seen, not just contacted.

What Is Included

Built Specifically for Healthcare

Every component of the system is designed for the compliance standards, patient journey, and operational realities of health service providers.

01 — Intake

Smart Intake Forms

Branching logic forms that adapt based on patient responses, capturing the right information the first time and reducing back-and-forth for your staff.

02 — Automation

Workflow Automation

Built on Make.com and n8n, our automation layer handles routing, notifications, follow-ups, and data entry — zero manual processing required.

03 — Intelligence

AI Lead Scoring

Proprietary scoring models assess intent, urgency, and fit so your team focuses exclusively on leads most likely to become active patients.

04 — Compliance

Data Privacy

All data handling meets UAE PDPL and international HIPAA standards. Patient data is encrypted, access-controlled, and never sold or shared.

05 — Scheduling

Booking Integration

Connects directly to your scheduling platform so qualified leads land as booked appointments — not just names in a spreadsheet.

06 — Analytics

Live Reporting

A real-time dashboard showing lead volume, channel performance, qualification rates, and appointment conversion — updated daily.

07 — Channels

Multi-Channel Reach

Capture leads from Google, Meta, WhatsApp, your website, and referral partnerships — all flowing into a single unified system.

Monthly reviews, A/B testing on forms and messaging, and continuous tuning of the qualification model to improve results over time.
