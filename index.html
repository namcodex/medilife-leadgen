What We Offer

Home Health Services in Dubai & UAE

Every service is delivered by DHA-certified professionals — to your home, hotel, or workplace. No queues. No waiting rooms.

Doctor on Call
Licensed home doctors available 24/7 — consultations, prescriptions, and follow-up visits at your location.
Home Nursing
Post-operative care, wound dressing, medication administration, and elderly caregiving — all at home.
IV Drip Therapy
Hydration drips, vitamin infusions, glutathione, NAD+, and hangover recovery — administered by trained nurses.
Blood Test at Home
Full-body checkups, PCR tests, STD screening, pregnancy blood tests — fast results, DHA-certified nurses.
Physiotherapy
Mobility improvement, pain relief, post-surgery rehabilitation — at-home physiotherapy tailored to your needs.
Vaccinations at Home
Flu shots, chickenpox vaccines, HPV, and more — administered safely at home by certified healthcare professionals.
Elderly Care
Compassionate daily care for elderly patients — bathing, feeding, companionship, Dementia and Alzheimer's support.
Air Ambulance
Medical evacuation and air ambulance services for time-critical patient transfers — global coverage.
